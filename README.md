## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains my **Advanced Prompt Engineering — Part B** work.

The project focuses on how **Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)** can assist with customer-support automation for an online food-delivery platform such as Zomato.

The portfolio contains **25 advanced prompts** covering business research, customer support, prompt engineering, structured analysis, verification, critical evaluation, and human-in-the-loop decision-making.

The portfolio follows the principle:

**Business Problem → Information → AI Task → Prompt → AI Output → Human Review → Business Decision**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The selected business problem is:

> **Customer-support automation using Generative AI / LLMs.**

The project investigates how AI can assist with routine customer queries while maintaining **human escalation for complex, sensitive, or high-impact issues**.

The research focuses on:

* Customer-support automation
* Routine query handling
* Complaint analysis
* AI-assisted support
* Human escalation
* Business impact
* Risks and limitations
* Evidence verification
* Responsible AI use

---

## ❓ Research Question

**How can AI-based customer-support automation improve response time and customer experience for Zomato while maintaining human escalation for complex complaints?**

## 🔄 Prompt Improvement

One important example of prompt refinement is:

### Basic Prompt

> "Tell me about AI in Zomato."

### Improved Prompt

The improved version adds:

* A defined role
* Business context
* Specific customer-support task
* Constraints
* Human escalation requirement
* Verification requirements
* Structured output format

This demonstrates how a vague prompt can be transformed into a more focused and research-oriented prompt.

---

## 🔍 Fact-Checking & Verification

AI-generated information is not automatically treated as verified evidence.

Important claims should be checked against credible sources such as:

* Official company sources
* Annual reports
* Government or regulatory sources
* Research papers
* University/research publications
* Established business publications

The project also distinguishes between:

**Evidence → Interpretation → Recommendation**

This helps reduce unsupported claims and clearly separates AI-generated analysis from independently verified information.

## 👤 Human-in-the-Loop Approach

AI is used as an **assistance and research-support tool**, not as the final decision-maker.

Human review is important for:

* Complex complaints
* Payment disputes
* Safety-related complaints
* Sensitive customer situations
* Ambiguous cases
* Important factual claims
* Final business decisions

This approach helps maintain human oversight while using AI for repetitive and structured tasks.
